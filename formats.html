Formats

Lossless keeps every detail of the original. Lossy discards what ears rarely notice to shrink the file.

FormatTypeGood for
.wavLosslessUncompressed studio audio; huge but exact.
.mp3LossyPlays everywhere; the safe default.
.oggLossyOpen Vorbis codec; popular in games.
.opusLossyBest quality at low bitrates; voice and streaming.
.flacLosslessCompressed but perfect; for music archives.
.aacLossyRaw AAC stream, common in radio.
.m4aLossyAAC in an MP4 wrapper; Apple's favorite.
.mp4LossyMP4 container carrying audio only.
.webmLossyWeb-native container with Opus.
.webaLossyAudio-only WebM; same codec, own extension.
